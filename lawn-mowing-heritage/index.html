Curb-appeal mowing tips for Heritage

  • Consistency beats catch-up. A steady weekly or biweekly cut keeps the lawn even and avoids the scalped look that comes from cutting an overgrown yard short.
  • Two grasses, two heights. Many yards mix sunny Bermuda (best around 1–2 inches) with St. Augustine in tree-shaded beds and front yards (roughly 3–4 inches).
  • Edges do the heavy lifting. Crisp lines along sidewalks, curbs and driveways make the biggest visual difference from the street.
  • Check your HOA rules. Guidelines vary, so look over your HOA’s yard standards and pick a mowing frequency that keeps you comfortably within them.
